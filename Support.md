## Getting Started

1. Install from the App Store and complete the first-launch flow (network check, optional tracking consent, and ad consent where required by your region).
2. From the main menu, tap **Play** to start. Hold to charge a jump; release to hop. **Tilt** your device to move left or right on platforms.
3. Collect coins, unlock skins in the **Shop**, track **Achievements**, and claim **Daily Rewards** when available.
4. Open **Settings** for sound, music, vibration, and links to the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
5. Optional: sign in to **Apple Game Center** to submit scores to leaderboards (governed by Apple’s policies).

For a quick in-app overview, open **Game Guide** from Settings.

## Troubleshooting

- **Tilt / motion control does not work:** Enable Motion & Fitness for Pixel Hop in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Motion & Fitness** (wording may vary by iOS version).
- **Character does not move as expected:** Calibrate by holding the device comfortably upright; avoid extreme angles or a flat table unless you prefer touch-only play where supported.
- **Ads do not load or stay blank:** Check Wi‑Fi or cellular data; try again later. Ad availability depends on your region and network. Declining tracking may show less relevant but still valid ads.
- **App stuck on launch or splash:** Force quit and reopen; ensure network is available on first install. If you are in the EU/EEA and consent dialogs appear, complete or dismiss them per on-screen instructions.
- **Progress or coins seem lost:** Progress is stored locally. Reinstalling the App or switching devices without backup may reset data. Device backups (e.g. iCloud) may restore data per Apple’s policies—we do not operate cloud save accounts in this version.
- **Game Center leaderboard issues:** Sign in to Game Center in iOS Settings; ensure you are using the same Apple ID as in the App.
- **Crashes or freezes:** Update to the latest App Store version; restart your device. If it persists, email us with device model, iOS version, and steps to reproduce.

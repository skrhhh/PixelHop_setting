# Pixel Hop Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** May 20, 2026  
**Last Updated:** May 20, 2026

The operator of the mobile application **Pixel Hop** (“we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect information related to your use of the App.

By using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

---

## 1. Scope

This Policy applies to information we and the third parties described below process when you download and use the App through the Apple App Store. It does not apply to third-party websites or services (for example, pages hosting this Policy, or ad landing pages), which are governed by their own policies.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide or That Is Generated Through Use

The App is primarily played offline. The following data is mainly stored **on your device** to support gameplay, settings, and experience:

- Game scores, high scores, and mode-specific records  
- In-game currency (coins) balance and usage (for in-app features only)  
- Unlocked/selected character skins and achievement progress  
- Preferences (sound, music, vibration, etc.)  
- Daily login and daily reward claim records  
- Tutorial completion status  
- Local counters related to ads (e.g. daily rewarded ad views for in-game rewards or limits)

This data generally **does not leave your device**, except where it may be included in **device backups** (e.g. iCloud) under Apple’s policies.

### 2.2 Automatically Collected Technical and Usage Information

To operate the App, analyze usage, display ads, and fix crashes, we may automatically collect information through third-party SDKs, including but not limited to:

- Device type, OS version, language/region settings  
- App version, session duration, aggregated usage events (e.g. screens/scenes, button interactions)  
- Advertising identifiers (where you grant permission, e.g. Apple IDFA; otherwise non-personalized identifiers may be used)  
- IP address (often processed by ad/analytics providers for regional stats, fraud prevention, etc.)  
- Crash logs, error stacks, and performance diagnostics  

We do **not** require your real name, government ID, payment card, precise home address, contacts, or photo library for the App. The current MVP version does **not** offer account registration or login.

### 2.3 Apple Game Center (If Used)

If you use Apple Game Center, we may, through Apple’s APIs:

- Submit game scores, total jumps, and similar game data to leaderboards  
- Display leaderboard and achievement-related features  

Game Center is governed by **Apple’s Privacy Policy**. We only receive data Apple provides for game functionality and do not ask for your Game Center password.

### 2.4 Device Permissions

The App may request or use the following only for stated purposes:

| Permission / Capability | Purpose |
|-------------------------|---------|
| Motion & Fitness / device motion | Tilt control via gyroscope |
| Tracking (App Tracking Transparency, if prompted) | More relevant ads (you may decline; the App remains usable; ads may be less relevant) |
| Network access | Load ads, send analytics/crash reports, open Privacy Policy / Terms links |

You can review or revoke permissions in iOS **Settings** at any time.
